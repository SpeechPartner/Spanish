English. This app is free and will stay free. Do you find it useful? You can buy the developer a coffee for 1.99 euro. This is entirely voluntary; you receive no product or service in return.

Payment goes through PayPal, an external payment provider. PayPal processes your payment details; the app receives nothing from it.
